My Promise To You

Every mistake has taught me something valuable.

Today, on your birthday, I don't just want to say "I'm sorry"— I want to promise that I'll become a better person.

I promise to respect your feelings, value your trust, listen with patience, and always wish the best for you.

Whether life keeps us close or takes us on different paths, I'll always pray for your happiness and never stop being grateful that I met you.

Thank you for being such a wonderful part of my life. ❤️
